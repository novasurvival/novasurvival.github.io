BUILD SYSTEM

Turn XP into a build.

Normal upgrades shape the rhythm of your run. Major upgrades can change the direction of the run itself.

LEVEL UP

Choose. Combine. Escalate.

Level-ups put a set of powers in front of you and force a decision. Pick sustain, additional projectiles, damage-focused options or utility, then keep building around the choices that fit the current run.

Lifesteal
Recover a small amount of health through direct hits.
Twin Stream
Add extra shots and increase the pressure your build can output.
Nova Ammo
Push projectile behavior toward stronger penetration and damage.
MAJOR UPGRADES

Big power. Real tradeoffs.

Major upgrades are built to feel different from ordinary level-up choices. They can open breathing room, accelerate progression or push a run into a more aggressive direction, but the strongest choices come with tradeoffs.

Shield Pulse
Taking damage can create knockback space around you, but it is not a free escape from every hit.
XP Overload
Accelerates levelling and build growth while changing the pacing of the run.
One-run identity
Major choices are designed to make one run feel meaningfully different from the next.

ENEMY DATABASE

Learn the threat before it reaches you.

Nova Survival's enemies do not all pressure you in the same way. Some chase, some attack from range, some dive straight into you, and some turn one mistake into a heavy hit. Learn their patterns and shape your build around the wave in front of you.

RANGED ENEMY

Void Core

A purple ranged enemy that keeps its distance and fires energy projectiles at you. It creates pressure from outside the main swarm, so standing still for too long is a bad idea.

RANGED FIRE POSITIONING
STANDARD ENEMY

Blaze Mech

The red frontline mech relentlessly chases the player and deals damage on contact. Simple behavior, constant pressure. Let too many surround you and the battlefield closes fast.

CHASE CONTACT DAMAGE
HIGH-SPEED ENEMY

Aqua Drone

A fast flying attacker that rushes directly at you and tries to slam into the player. Its speed makes it dangerous, but a well-positioned drone build can remove it quickly.

FAST DIVE DRONE COUNTER
EXPLOSIVE ENEMY

Nova Guard

A green explosive unit that suddenly lunges toward you and detonates at close range. Its explosion deals heavy damage, making reaction time and spacing especially important.

LUNGE HIGH EXPLOSIVE DAMAGE
BOSS ENEMY

Elite Mech

A high-health boss with dangerous contact damage and a three-projectile ranged attack. Taking it down demands more focus, but it rewards the risk by dropping significantly more XP.
