Nova Survival ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Nova Survival on Android or iOS.

Nova Survival does not require a Nova Survival account and does not operate its own server-side user-profile database. The Game uses platform and advertising services that may process data to provide ads, purchases, consent management, security, diagnostics, and related functionality.

1. Data processed by the Game and its service providers

Depending on your platform, region, device settings, app version, privacy choices, and the features you use, the following data may be collected and shared with service providers:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
  • Device, app, and advertising identifiers where available and permitted.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, hangs, energy use, crashes, and SDK performance.
  • Consent and privacy-choice signals.
  • Purchase and transaction information needed to complete, verify, acknowledge, restore, and prevent abuse of in-app purchases. We do not receive complete payment-card details.

At first setup in every country, the slider offers 1950 through the current year, with no preselected answer. Only the selected birth year is saved locally; no month, day or raw year is sent to a developer server or advertising provider. The current calendar year minus the selected year determines the approximate age: 13 or younger receives child treatment, 14–17 receives under-age treatment, and 18 or older receives adult treatment. Unknown or invalid ages receive child treatment. All ads remain nonpersonalized and G-rated. The local year is excluded from app backup. Old app-owned age bands and optional-ad switches are removed without resetting Google choices.

Google states that data automatically collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit and is used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

2. Advertising, AdMob, age treatment, and consent

After both opening steps, UMP requests one current consent-information update per app session; under-age treatment is enabled for players under 18 or with no year specified. This network check may process technical information such as IP address before an ad is loaded. The app uses the current SDK response, not language or SIM country. A Google form is shown only when the SDK requires it; required Google privacy options remain available in the main-menu settings. Ads require a successful fresh check and UMP canRequestAds. Publisher personalization and publisher first-party ID are disabled, and iOS does not request Apple tracking authorization.

Rewarded ads are optional. A player chooses whether to watch a rewarded ad in exchange for the clearly stated in-game reward.

3. In-app purchases

Optional digital purchases are processed by Google Play Billing on Android and StoreKit/App Store on iOS. Google or Apple processes payment information under its own terms and privacy policy. Nova Survival does not collect or store full credit-card, debit-card, or bank-account information.

The Game receives only the product and transaction status needed to unlock content, acknowledge or restore eligible purchases, and reduce purchase fraud. Purchase entitlements may be stored locally on the device. If a player has not reached the age at which they may make purchases independently under applicable law, a parent or legal guardian must authorize each purchase.

On Android, selected progress information may be included in Android or Google device backup and restore services when those services are enabled. Clearing app data or uninstalling the Game removes local app data, but a platform backup may later restore eligible data according to your Google backup settings.

On iOS, progress and settings may be stored in UserDefaults, and a compact recovery copy may be stored in the iOS Keychain. Keychain information can survive a reinstall on the same device and may be included in an encrypted device migration or backup, depending on Apple's platform behavior and your settings.

The developer does not receive this local progress in an independent Nova Survival cloud account.

5. Third-party services

Nova Survival uses or may rely on:

  • Google AdMob and Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • Google Play services and Google Play Billing on Android.
  • Apple StoreKit, App Store, Keychain, and platform services on iOS.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies, terms, security practices, and legal obligations. Google privacy information is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Apple privacy information is available at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Nova Survival does not include Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics in the audited app version and does not independently sell personal information. Advertising disclosures under some US laws may use broader terms such as "sale", "sharing", or "targeted advertising"; applicable opt-out choices are provided through UMP and platform controls where required.

6. Legal bases and regional rights

Where applicable, advertising processing is based on consent, legitimate interests, contractual necessity, or compliance with law, depending on the purpose, region, and service-provider role.

Users in the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or request portability of personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal.

Residents of applicable US states may have rights to know, access, correct, or delete personal data and to opt out of certain sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or profiling. Nova Survival will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

Most advertising data is controlled directly by Google rather than stored in a Nova Survival account. Use the in-game privacy entry point and your Google, Android, Apple, or iOS controls first. You may also contact us at the address below; we will respond to verifiable requests within the period required by applicable law and explain when a request must be directed to a service provider.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until it is cleared, overwritten, or removed, subject to Android backup or iOS Keychain and encrypted-backup behavior described above. You can clear local Android app data or uninstall the Game. On iOS, uninstalling may not remove Keychain recovery data; resetting the device Keychain or contacting us for guidance may be necessary.

Google and Apple retain information according to their own policies, user controls, and legal obligations. Advertising identifiers can be reset or deleted through supported device or account controls. Eligible users can change available consent choices through the in-game "Privacy and Cookie Settings" entry point when UMP requires it.

8. Children and teens

Nova Survival is rated PEGI 7 and is intended for a mixed audience aged 7 and older. It is not intended for children under 7. A content rating does not determine who may legally accept terms, consent to data processing, or make purchases; players below the applicable age of legal majority must use the Game with appropriate parent or legal-guardian authorization and supervision.

9. Security

Google states that Google Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Purchases are handled through Google Play or Apple's App Store. We use reasonable safeguards within the Game, but no transmission, device, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game, service providers, legal requirements, age-treatment practices, or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at the same public URL with a revised "Last Updated" date.
