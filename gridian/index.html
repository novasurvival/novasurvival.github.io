Privacy policy

Document version 8 · 29 October 2026

Scope and local records

This notice covers Gridian: Block Puzzle 1.0.0 for Android and iOS. No player account, developer game server, developer analytics service, cloud synchronization or real-money in-app purchase is provided. Scores, progress, virtual items, sound/language settings and privacy choices are stored locally. The game does not request contacts, precise location, camera or microphone access.

The short opening screen

First choose a language, then read the linked Privacy policy, Cookies & local storage notice and Terms of use and choose I agree to continue. A separate birth-year step follows. Accepting the documents is not permission for personalized advertising or tracking and does not replace Google choices required in your region.

Birth year and protective advertising

At first setup in every country, the slider offers 1950 through the current year, with no preselected answer. Only the selected birth year is saved locally; no month, day or raw year is sent to a developer server or advertising provider. The current calendar year minus the selected year determines the approximate age: 13 or younger receives child treatment, 14–17 receives under-age treatment, and 18 or older receives adult treatment. Unknown or invalid ages receive child treatment. All ads remain nonpersonalized and G-rated. The local year is excluded from app backup. Old app-owned age bands and optional-ad switches are removed without resetting Google choices.

Google consent and advertising

After both opening steps, UMP requests one current consent-information update per app session; under-age treatment is enabled for players under 18 or with no year specified. This network check may process technical information such as IP address before an ad is loaded. The app uses the current SDK response, not language or SIM country. A Google form is shown only when the SDK requires it; required Google privacy options remain available in the main-menu settings. Ads require a successful fresh check and UMP canRequestAds. Publisher personalization and publisher first-party ID are disabled, and iOS does not request Apple tracking authorization. This test version uses official Google sample ad units. There is no separate app control to turn ads off or reject the accepted documents. Google choices and service availability still govern whether an ad can be served; a failed check does not block gameplay.

Data processed by Google

Nonpersonalized and test ads are not data-free. Google may process IP address and approximate location derived from it, device/app information and identifiers, consent signals, diagnostics and ad interactions for delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Google describes its purposes, providers and retention under its own disclosures. Google Mobile Ads describes TLS protection for its transmitted data; this is not a claim that every provider or consent flow was independently audited. The publisher does not receive local game records through a developer game server. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads .

Your controls and regional rights

You can read the documents and contact the publisher from the main-menu settings. When required, Google's privacy options allow you to review or change the choices offered by Google, including applicable regional or Do Not Sell or Share choices. The app does not replace, suppress or reset those provider choices. General document acceptance is separate from them. New ad requests and presentation are stopped while the app is in the background or covered by a privacy document or form. Previously submitted network operations may finish, but stale results are not used. An initialized SDK can remain active until the app process ends.

Retention, deletion and contact

Local records remain until you clear app data or uninstall; Android backup is disabled, while iOS backups may retain or restore local records under your device settings. Google controls retention of the data it receives. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal or an appeal, and complain to the relevant data protection authority. We do not sell local game records. Because no player account or developer server copy exists, we may be unable to identify data solely on your device. Do not send identity documents unnecessarily. Parents and players can use Settings → Support in the app for privacy requests or support. Publisher: Borderline Interactive.

Local reminders and support

After initial gameplay, the game may ask the operating system for notification permission. If permitted, two return messages alternate about every 48 hours while you are away. Returning to the game resets the schedule. Delivery can be delayed or suppressed by system settings, battery controls, force-stop or the finite iOS pending schedule. The app uses local scheduling, not a push server, remote token or notification analytics. Permission and reminders can be controlled in device notification settings. Sound, music and haptics can be changed in the game. For support, open Settings → Support in the app and use the contact method provided there. Please do not include unnecessary sensitive information.

The information website

These documents are also available offline in the game. The public information site adds no publisher-authored advertising cookies or analytics; its separate hosting platform may process technical access data under its own practices. Visiting this website does not enable ads or change privacy choices in the mobile game. Notices may change with a new build; advertising choices are collected again when required.
